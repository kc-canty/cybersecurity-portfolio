# Lab Guidelines

Each lab in this portfolio follows a consistent structure to ensure clarity, repeatability, and realistic scope.

## Standard Lab Structure
Each lab directory contains:
- `README.md` – Lab objective, steps taken, and conclusions
- `evidence/` – Screenshots or logs collected during the lab
- `findings.md` – Reflections, challenges, and future improvements

## Time Scope
- Beginner labs are designed to be completed in 60–75 minutes
- Enterprise-context labs may span multiple sessions

## Documentation Philosophy
- Documentation focuses on *why* actions were taken, not just *what* was clicked
- Labs are not polished to production standards
- Trade-offs and limitations are acknowledged where appropriate

## Ethical Statement
All labs were completed in isolated, non-production environments owned by the author.
